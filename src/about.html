Toshkentdagi Prezident maktabi

O‘zbekiston Respublikasi tashabbusi bilan tashkil etilgan mazkur yetakchi ta’lim muassasasi iqtidorli va iqtidorli yoshlarni aniqlash, qo‘llab-quvvatlash va kamol toptirishga qaratilgan. Maktabda ta’lim zamonaviy pedagogik yondashuvlar, ilg‘or o‘quv dasturlari va yuqori malakali o‘qituvchilar mehnati asosida olib borilmoqda.

Our Vision

The vision of the Presidential Schools is to prepare the next generation of leaders to actively and positively contribute to the development of Uzbekistan by providing educational opportunities that encourage the nation's gifted and talented youth to realize their potential as creative, innovative participants in a dynamic global society

Our Mission

The mission of the Presidential Schools is to enhance the educational experience for gifted students through a programme of academic excellence and holistic learning opportunities to develop the leadership potential of its graduates to contribute to the future of Uzbekistan.
